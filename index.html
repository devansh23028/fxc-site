FAQ

Quick answers for customers.

How do I change prices?

Edit the price and oldPrice fields in products.js.

How do I add my QR code?

Put your QR image inside the images folder and set its filename in the product's qr field.

How do I change an offer?

Edit the offer field. You can write things such as “30% OFF” or “NEW”.

Can I add more products?

Yes. Duplicate a product object in products.js and change its details.
